## 6. Risk of Bias (RoB 2) — for Day 28 mortality outcome

| Domain | Judgement | Support |
|--------|----------|---------|
| D1: Randomization process | Low risk | Computer-generated, blocks of 8, sequentially numbered sealed envelopes. Baseline characteristics well balanced. |
| D2: Deviations from intended interventions | Some concerns | Completely open-label. Airway Team knew allocation. Critical care teams also unblinded. All subsequent ICU care at discretion of treating teams. Could influence co-interventions. However, adherence was excellent (99%+ received assigned drug). |
| D3: Missing outcome data | Low risk | 10/801 (1.2%) post-enrollment withdrawals (4 etomidate, 6 ketamine). Survival data verified post-trial by team members blinded to allocation. |
| D4: Measurement of the outcome | Low risk | Day 28 mortality is objective. Verified by medical records and blinded team members. |
| D5: Selection of reported result | Low risk | Pre-registered NCT02643381 (December 2015). Primary endpoint (Day 7 survival) unchanged since initial registration. SAP finalized August 2020 before analysis. Day 28 mortality was pre-specified secondary. |
| **Overall** | **Some concerns** | **Due to open-label design (D2). Single-center trial with specialized Airway Team — may limit generalizability but does not directly bias mortality.** |

## 7. Notes

- Primary endpoint in original trial was **Day 7 survival** (unusual endpoint), not Day 28. Day 7 significantly favored ketamine (p=0.005), but this signal disappeared by Day 28 (p=0.294). Authors acknowledge this early separation with subsequent convergence is difficult to interpret.
- **Single-center** trial at UT-Southwestern (academic Level-1 Trauma center) with specialized anesthesiology-based Airway Team — limits generalizability.
- Cardiovascular collapse (Vanderbilt Definition) was HIGHER with ketamine (25.1% vs 17.4%) — consistent with Casey 2025 finding.
- Low actual ketamine dose: median 1.1 mg/kg (IQR 0.8–1.5) — lower than the 2 mg/kg typical dose and lower than Casey (1.6 mg/kg) or Jabre (2 mg/kg).
- Trial closed early by DSMB (not for efficacy/futility, but because enrolled minus withdrawals was substantially <750). Underpowered for Day 28.
- EFIC trial (Exception from Informed Consent).
- For NMA: use **142/396 (etomidate) vs 131/395 (ketamine)** for 28-day mortality.
