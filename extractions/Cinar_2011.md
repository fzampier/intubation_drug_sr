## 6. Risk of Bias (RoB 2) — for mortality outcome

| Domain | Judgement | Support |
|--------|----------|---------|
| D1: Randomization process | Some concerns | Web-based random number generator used. Syringes pre-prepared and sequentially numbered. However, very small sample (n=22); APACHE II numerically higher in ketamine group (32.1 vs 28.7, p=0.144). Allocation concealment appears adequate (numbered syringes, clinician blinded). |
| D2: Deviations from intended interventions | Low risk | Double-blind: identical syringe volumes, content blinded to intubator and ICU staff. All intubations performed by same person (O.C.). |
| D3: Missing outcome data | Low risk | 2 patients excluded during follow-up (received steroids during ICU stay) but all 22 initially randomized were accounted for in mortality analysis. |
| D4: Measurement of the outcome | Low risk | Mortality is objective. |
| D5: Selection of reported result | Some concerns | No trial registration. Primary outcome was hemodynamic response, not mortality. Mortality reported as secondary outcome. |
| **Overall** | **Some concerns** | **Small trial with no registration. Mortality was secondary; potential for selective reporting. Double-blind is a strength.** |

## 7. Notes

- **FLAG FOR SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS:** Ketamine arm received midazolam 0.03 mg/kg adjunct — this is a co-intervention not applied to the etomidate arm.
- Extremely small trial (n=22). Very limited statistical power for mortality.
- No NMBA was used in either arm — this is unique among included studies.
- ARDS developed in 10/12 (83%) etomidate vs 2/10 (20%) ketamine patients (p=0.008) — striking but likely driven by small sample.
- ICU mortality used as best available (no 28-day mortality reported).
- Cortisol and 11β-deoxycortisol measured at baseline and 5 min post-intubation — no significant difference between groups.
- SOFA scores followed for 9 days — no significant difference.
- For NMA: use 5/12 (etomidate) vs 8/10 (ketamine) for ICU mortality.
- Published in Turkish journal with English abstract/summary; main text in Turkish.
