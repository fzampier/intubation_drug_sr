**Key inclusion criteria:**
- Adults ≥18 years
- Undergoing RSI (near-simultaneous administration of sedative + NMBA) in the ED
- Critically ill

**Key exclusion criteria (after protocol change; first 103 enrolled under original criteria):**
- Condition where increased HR or BP hazardous (e.g., aneurysmal SAH, hypertensive emergency)
- Known or suspected increased intracranial pressure
- Known allergy to ketamine or etomidate
- Wearing "KvE declined" opt-out bracelet
- Prisoners or under arrest
- Female patients of childbearing age (18–50) without documented negative pregnancy test
- (Original criteria for first 103 patients: only allergy, bracelet, and prisoner exclusions)

## 6. Risk of Bias (RoB 2) — for 30-day mortality outcome

| Domain | Judgement | Support |
|--------|----------|---------|
| D1: Randomization process | Low risk | Computer-generated assignment sequence in permuted blocks of 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10. Sequentially numbered opaque envelopes. Baseline characteristics reasonably balanced (some imbalances in sepsis prevalence: 14% vs 26%). |
| D2: Deviations from intended interventions | Some concerns | ED team unblinded; ICU team blinded (drug not in medication administration record). 5 crossovers (3 ketamine→etomidate, 2 etomidate→ketamine) based on clinical judgment. Post-intubation care at discretion of treating physician. ITT analysis used. |
| D3: Missing outcome data | Low risk | 14/143 (9.8%) withdrew from study. However, under FDA 21 CFR 50.24, mortality could be obtained via public records for all patients. Mortality data available for all 143 (ITT). Complete SOFA data available for 129/143 (90.2%). |
| D4: Measurement of the outcome | Low risk | 30-day mortality is objective. Ascertained by trained research staff blinded to group assignment via chart review and public records. |
| D5: Selection of reported result | Some concerns | **Primary outcome changed during the trial**: originally mortality at hospital discharge/30 days for first 103 patients; changed to maximum SOFA score after FDA mandate (IND submission). Exclusion criteria also changed mid-trial. Pre-registered NCT01823328 but protocol modifications occurred. Mortality reported as exploratory. |
| **Overall** | **Some concerns** | **D2 (partially blinded with crossovers) and D5 (primary endpoint changed mid-trial; exclusion criteria changed after first 103 patients).** |

## 7. Notes

- **Primary endpoint was max SOFA score** (not mortality) — changed mid-trial per FDA requirement for EFIC studies. For first 103 patients, mortality was primary; for remaining 40, max SOFA was primary.
- Full publication of **Driver 2014** conference abstract (same trial, NCT01823328). Driver 2014 was an interim analysis.
- Small trial (N=143) — limited power for mortality. The 10% absolute difference (11% vs 21%) is clinically large but not statistically significant.
- **Mixed ED population** — only 20% sepsis, 20% trauma, 20% overdose. Not restricted to any single diagnosis.
- Young cohort: median age 50 years — youngest in the review.
- High first-pass success (94% ketamine, 89% etomidate) — consistent with other trials.
- Fixed dose protocol: ketamine 2 mg/kg, etomidate 0.3 mg/kg — no dose adjustment allowed.
- Predominantly succinylcholine (90–93%) for NMBA.
- EFIC trial — no individual consent before randomization; opt-out bracelets available.
- Exclusion criteria changed mid-trial after first 103 patients (FDA mandated additional exclusions for ICP, hypertensive emergencies, childbearing age).
- Imbalance in sepsis at baseline (14% vs 26%) and difficult airway characteristics (64% vs 45%) — could confound.
- **Hypoxemia** (O₂ sat <90% within 5 min of intubation): 8/67 (12%) ketamine vs 14/72 (19%) etomidate, difference −8 (−19 to 4) — not significant.
- For NMA: use **8/70 (ketamine) vs 15/73 (etomidate)** for 30-day mortality.
