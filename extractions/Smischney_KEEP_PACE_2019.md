**Key inclusion criteria:**
- Adults ≥18 years
- Admitted to medical, surgical, or oncologic/transplant ICU
- Requiring emergent intubation (without delay)

**Key exclusion criteria:**
- Intubation for cardiac arrest
- Intracranial pathology with known/suspected elevated ICP
- Chronic opioid dependence
- Bipolar/schizophrenia
- Egg allergy
- Contraindications to study drugs
- Weight >140 kg or <30 kg
- Previously enrolled
- Continuous sedative infusions in prior 24h
- Procedural intubation
- Incarceration
- Women 18–50 without pregnancy test

*Emergency stopping rules (5 min pre-drug): HR >160 or <50; SBP >180 or <70; DBP >120 or <30.*

## 6. Risk of Bias (RoB 2) — for mortality outcome

| Domain | Judgement | Support |
|--------|----------|---------|
| D1: Randomization process | Low risk | Computer-generated randomization, stratified by unit and shock status, sealed opaque envelopes. Block size unknown to investigators. Baseline characteristics balanced except cardiac medications and prior intubations. |
| D2: Deviations from intended interventions | Some concerns | Open-label intervention. Co-interventions (midazolam, NMBAs, opioids, sedation) were noncontrolled. Outcome adjudicators and statistician were blinded. ~20% in each group required rescue doses. |
| D3: Missing outcome data | Low risk | 8/160 (5%) withdrew consent. 152 analyzed. Mortality ascertainable from hospital records. |
| D4: Measurement of the outcome | Low risk | Hospital mortality is objective. |
| D5: Selection of reported result | Low risk | Pre-registered (NCT02105415). Endpoints pre-specified. Independent DSMB oversight with quarterly reviews. |
| **Overall** | **Some concerns** | **Open-label design (D2) with noncontrolled co-interventions. However, mortality outcome unlikely affected by lack of blinding. Trial well-conducted with DSMB oversight.** |

## 7. Notes

- This is the **only trial providing the Ketofol–Etomidate edge** in the NMA network.
- Both study drug doses were REDUCED from standard clinical doses (etomidate 0.15 vs usual 0.3 mg/kg; KPA 0.5+0.5 vs usual higher doses).
- Fentanyl 50 µg was given to all patients in both arms as standard co-induction.
- High sepsis prevalence (~78% in both groups) but mixed ICU population.
- Primary endpoint was hemodynamic (MAP change at 5 min), not mortality — trial was not powered for mortality.
- Immediate adrenal insufficiency significantly more common with etomidate (81% vs 38%, p=0.027) but only in subset tested.
- Non-RBC transfusion higher with etomidate (22% vs 10%, p=0.046).
- For NMA: use 25/79 (ketofol) vs 26/73 (etomidate) for hospital mortality.
