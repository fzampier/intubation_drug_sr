## 6. Risk of Bias (RoB 2) — for 28-day mortality outcome

| Domain | Judgement | Support |
|--------|----------|---------|
| D1: Randomization process | Low risk | Computerized random-number generator, blocks of 4, stratified by center, sequentially numbered identical sealed drug boxes. |
| D2: Deviations from intended interventions | Some concerns | Single-blind: enrolling emergency physicians aware of allocation; ICU staff masked. Post-intubation sedation standardized. Could affect peri-intubation co-interventions. |
| D3: Missing outcome data | Some concerns | mITT excluded 27.6% of randomized patients (181/655): 27 died pre-hospital (12+15) + 154 discharged from ICU <3 days. ITT (n=650) showed consistent result (p=0.54), mitigating concern. |
| D4: Measurement of the outcome | Low risk | 28-day mortality is objective. Recorded from ICU records. |
| D5: Selection of reported result | Low risk | Pre-registered NCT00440102. Primary endpoint was maximum SOFA (not mortality). 28-day mortality was pre-specified secondary. No evidence of selective reporting. |
| **Overall** | **Some concerns** | **D2 (partial blinding) and D3 (mITT excluding 28% of randomized patients, though ITT consistent).** |

## 7. Notes

- Primary endpoint of original trial was maximum SOFA score (not mortality) — favored etomidate numerically (10.3 vs 9.6, p=0.056).
- mITT analysis excluded patients dying before hospital and those discharged from ICU within 3 days. This is unusual and potentially biasing (informative exclusion).
- ITT mortality: consistent with mITT (2% difference, p=0.54).
- Adrenal insufficiency significantly higher with etomidate: OR 6.7 (95% CI 3.5–12.7).
- Prehospital enrollment (EMS setting) — may differ from in-hospital ED/ICU setting of other included studies.
- All patients received succinylcholine (no rocuronium).
- Fixed doses (0.3 mg/kg etomidate, 2 mg/kg ketamine) — strict protocol, no dose adjustment.
- For NMA: use 81/234 (etomidate) vs 72/235 (ketamine) for 28-day mortality.
