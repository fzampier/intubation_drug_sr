## 6. Risk of Bias (RoB 2) — for mortality outcome

| Domain | Judgement | Support |
|--------|----------|---------|
| D1: Randomization process | Low risk | Permuted blocks, stratified by site, sequentially numbered opaque envelopes concealed until after enrollment. Baseline characteristics well balanced. |
| D2: Deviations from intended interventions | Some concerns | Unblinded — clinicians aware of assignment. Could influence co-interventions (vasopressor management, sedation choices). However, adherence was excellent (99.2% and 99.6% received assigned drug). |
| D3: Missing outcome data | Low risk | Only 6/2,365 (0.3%) withdrew before 28-day ascertainment. Death ascertained via medical record, vital statistics, and phone calls at 3 and 12 months. |
| D4: Measurement of the outcome | Low risk | Mortality is objective. Ascertained from multiple sources (medical records, vital statistics, family contact). |
| D5: Selection of reported result | Low risk | Pre-registered (NCT05277896). Protocol and SAP published before enrollment ended (ref 48, CHEST Crit Care 2025). Primary outcome clearly pre-specified. |
| **Overall** | **Some concerns** | **Due to open-label design (D2). However, primary outcome (mortality) is unlikely to be influenced by lack of blinding.** |

## 7. Notes

- Largest trial in the review (N=2,365). Contributes ~48% of total sample.
- Exception from Informed Consent (EFIC) — no individual consent required before randomization.
- Pragmatic design: clinicians chose dose within provided ranges and managed all other care per usual practice.
- Cardiovascular collapse was HIGHER with ketamine (22.1% vs 17.0%), driven mainly by vasopressor escalation — opposite of expected hemodynamic benefit.
- Sepsis subgroup (n=1,101): mortality 38.8% vs 38.2%, RD 1.0 pp (−4.8 to 6.7) — no difference.
- Vasopressors at baseline subgroup: mortality 46.7% vs 49.8% — no interaction.
- Ventricular tachycardia (post hoc): 9/884 (1.0%) vs 2/905 (0.2%), RD 0.8 pp (0.1 to 1.5) — signal of concern with ketamine.
- For NMA: use 330/1,173 (ketamine) vs 345/1,186 (etomidate) for primary mortality outcome.
