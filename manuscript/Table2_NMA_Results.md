**Notes:**

OR = odds ratio; CI = confidence interval; NMA = network meta-analysis; E = etomidate; K = ketamine.

*Panel A:* All comparisons from the four-node random-effects NMA (etomidate, ketamine, ketofol, propofol). Etomidate vs propofol is entirely indirect (through the ketamine node). OR < 1 favors the first-named treatment (lower mortality). Certainty assessed using the CINeMA framework.

*Panel B:* OR > 1 indicates higher event rate with ketamine; OR < 1 indicates lower event rate with ketamine. Estimates from NMA where the network included ≥3 treatments; pairwise random-effects meta-analysis where only etomidate and ketamine were compared (vasopressor 24 hours). Direct E vs K denotes studies directly comparing etomidate and ketamine; remaining studies in the NMA contributed indirect evidence through propofol or ketofol nodes.

*Vasopressor timepoints:* peri-intubation (induction to 2–5 minutes) and 24 hours. The 24-hour vasopressor estimate showed substantial heterogeneity (I² = 93%); individual study ORs ranged from 1.15 (Casey 2025) to 3.86 (Agarwal 2025), likely reflecting differences in population severity and outcome definitions. This result should be interpreted with caution.

*Certainty of evidence:* Assessed using the CINeMA framework (Nikolakopoulou et al., 2020) and GRADE guidance for NMA. See supplementary GRADE tables for detailed domain-level assessments.
